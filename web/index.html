    About this map

    I collect plane crash records from Wikipedia and link to their articles and available images.

    Why some records have markers

    A marker requires event-specific coordinates in Wikipedia’s Site field. I use an accident article’s Site field. Nearby towns, departure airports, memorials, and last-known positions are not used as substitutes. Dashed marker outlines indicate approximate crash sites.

    Records without event-site coordinates are available in All records. Accidents and incidents can have markers; records without Site coordinates remain searchable. The dataset is incomplete, especially for military crashes and older records.

    Use custom years and the decade dropdown together to filter dates. Marker color can show fatalities, year, or a single color. Unrecorded or ambiguous fatality counts are gray; colors use the event’s reported total, not a separate count for each aircraft.

    Images may show the aircraft, crash site, or aftermath. Their file pages provide credits and licenses. Wikipedia text is reused under CC BY-SA 4.0.
