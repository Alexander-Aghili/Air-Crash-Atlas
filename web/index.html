    About this map

    We collect plane crash records from Wikipedia and link to their articles and available images.

    Why some records have markers

    A marker requires coordinates for the crash site. We use an accident article’s Site field or a separately sourced location. Nearby towns, departure airports, memorials, and last-known positions are not used as substitutes. Dashed marker outlines indicate approximate crash sites.

    Records without crash-site coordinates are available in All records. Incidents and disappearances may have no crash site to map. The dataset is incomplete, especially for military crashes and older records.

    Use custom years and the decade dropdown together to filter dates. Marker color can show fatalities, year, or a single color. Unrecorded or ambiguous fatality counts are gray; colors use the event’s reported total, not a separate count for each aircraft.

    Images may show the aircraft, crash site, or aftermath. Their file pages provide credits and licenses. Separately sourced OpenStreetMap wreck-site coordinates are approximate and reused under ODbL. Wikipedia text is reused under CC BY-SA 4.0.
